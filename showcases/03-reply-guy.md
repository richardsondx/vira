# Showcase 03: Reply-guy arbitrage

**Question:** Can replies alone grow a mid-size account to 40k?

**Method:** VIRA map_engagement_strategy: reply graph over 90 days, reply timing relative to parent post, reply-to-follower conversion proxy.

**Findings (illustrative structure):**

1. **Volume and timing:** 10 targeted replies per day to accounts over 100k in the same niche, 80% within 30 minutes of the parent post. First-reply positioning correlated with 3x median reply likes.

2. **Format:** value-add, not praise. Patterns: a concrete number, a counter-example, a 3-bullet expansion. Median reply length 180-240 characters.

3. **Target selection:** 15 rotating large accounts, not random. Replies clustered around topics where the replier had genuine expertise.

4. **Conversion:** profile visits spiked on days with 2+ high-performing replies. Bio and pinned tweet were optimized to convert that traffic: clear niche, social proof, one CTA.

**Evidence required:** reply URLs, parent post URLs and timestamps, reply like counts, daily follower deltas.

**Unknowns:** reply-to-follower conversion cannot be measured directly from public data. Private profile-visit analytics are account-owner only.

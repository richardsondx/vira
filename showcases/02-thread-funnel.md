# Showcase 02: The 10k to 100k thread funnel

**Question:** What thread cadence actually moves a finance or startup account from 10k to 100k?

**Method:** VIRA trace_growth_levers over 180 days. Isolate top 5% posts by engagement, classify triggers.

**Findings (illustrative structure):**

1. **Cadence:** 2 long threads per week outperformed 7 short posts per week for follower growth. Threads averaged 8-12 tweets, hook under 12 words, CTA in final tweet.

2. **Hook archetypes that recurred in outliers:**
   - "I spent X years doing Y. Here is what I learned:"
   - "Most people misunderstand Z. A thread:"
   - Number-led: "7 mistakes that cost me $X"

3. **Distribution:** one viral thread per month drove an estimated 60% of net follower growth. Non-viral threads maintained baseline engagement and trained the voice.

4. **Repurposing:** each thread was atomized into 5-8 single posts over the following 2 weeks, extending its half-life.

**Evidence required:** thread URLs, per-tweet engagement, posting timestamps, follower counts before and after (Social Blade snapshots).

**Unknowns:** X does not expose per-post follower attribution publicly. Estimates come from follower-count deltas around post timestamps, which conflate multiple causes.

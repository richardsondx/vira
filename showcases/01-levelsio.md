# Showcase 01: @levelsio, build-in-public as a compounding loop

**Question:** How does a solo indie hacker turn monthly revenue screenshots into durable follower growth?

**Method:** VIRA collect over 120 days, top 200 posts by engagement. Pattern-mine across post-type taxonomy, cadence, and reply graph.

**Findings (illustrative structure, fill with fetched data):**

1. **Three post types form the flywheel:**
   - MRR and milestone screenshots (1-2 per month): highest like-to-follower ratio, drives profile visits
   - Contrarian hot-takes on startups and remote work (2-3 per week): highest reply rate, drives distribution via quote-tweets
   - Build updates and travel photos (daily): baseline cadence, keeps account active in algorithm

2. **Discovery lever:** early replies to large tech accounts within 30 minutes. Value-add format: a concrete number or a counter-example, not praise.

3. **Hook fingerprint:** under 12 words, specific number or strong claim in first 8 words, no hashtags.

**Evidence required:** each claim cites 3-5 post URLs with like, repost, reply counts and timestamps.

**Replicable playbook excerpt:** 1 milestone post per month with a real number, 2 hot-takes per week in your niche, daily build update, 5 targeted replies per day to accounts over 100k in your niche.

**Unknowns:** true follower attribution per post is not measurable from public data. Impression counts are account-private.
